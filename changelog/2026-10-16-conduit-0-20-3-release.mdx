[Conduit v0.20.3](https://github.com/ConduitIO/conduit/releases/tag/v0.20.3) is a patch release. It fixes a panic in the `split` processor and a 60-second stall on graceful shutdown with destination SDK batching.

<!--truncate-->

### Key Highlights

- **`split` no longer panics on a nested field.** A discarded resolve error after `Record.Clone` could crash the engine; the panic is fixed and the resolve error is surfaced.
- **Graceful shutdown exits promptly with SDK batching.** When the last record of a drain exactly filled an SDK batch, shutdown could stall for its full 60-second timeout (invariant 7). Fixed by bumping the connector SDK to v0.14.4.
