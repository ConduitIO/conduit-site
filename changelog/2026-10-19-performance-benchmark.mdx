Conduit v0.21 ships a connector write-path performance sweep and a reproducible
CDC benchmark against Kafka Connect + Debezium.

<!--truncate-->

### Key Highlights

- **A reproducible benchmark is now in the repo.** [`benchi/v021-cdc-parity`](https://github.com/ConduitIO/conduit/tree/main/benchi/v021-cdc-parity) compares Conduit, Kafka Connect + Debezium, and Redpanda Connect on the same Postgres CDC workload, with an A/A noise floor and ground-truth metrics (`wc -l`, `SELECT count(*)`, topic offsets) instead of engine instrumentation. Run it yourself with `terraform apply` + `bash run.sh`.
- **Postgres destination: +36.4% at parity defaults.** Conduit's default config (memoized SQL, a schema-guarded `cache_statement` exec mode, and a 3000-row destination batch) beats Debezium → JDBC sink on the `CDC→Postgres` workload, where the pre-fix defaults were ~47% behind.
- **File sink: +6.8%**, where the sink is the bottleneck.
- **Every certified connector swept for write-path overhead** — postgres, mysql, kafka, s3, elasticsearch, and the connector SDK — with fixes for per-record SQL/request rebuilds, missing default batching, and suboptimal client defaults, so the *defaults* are fast, not just a tuned config.

Full numbers and methodology on the [performance page](/performance).
